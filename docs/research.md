# Keji Visuals — research notes (23 Oct 2026)

## Design references (all opened in a browser)
| Reference | What we took |
|---|---|
| [ethanwong.photography](https://ethanwong.photography/) (Awwwards HM 2026) | Category list as hero navigation; chapter index on wedding galleries; **pricing page** structure (from-prices per shoot type, 3-step process, payment split, turnaround, "what changes the price"); mobile floating bottom bar with accent "Inquire". Avoid: preloader, tiny mobile thumbnails. |
| [almirakho.com](https://almirakho.com/) | One-line positioning statement as the "about". Avoid: text over busy photos on mobile. |
| [perappelgren.de](https://perappelgren.de/) | Generous whitespace makes images feel precious. Too sparse for a booking business. |
| [remyshoots.co.za](https://www.remyshoots.co.za/) | Focus image in colour, neighbours desaturated; view toggles; filter counts. Avoid: "enter with sound" gate. |
| [HENSON.STUDIO (Behance concept)](https://www.behance.net/gallery/139601119/HENSONSTUDIO-Photographer-Portfolio-Website) | Strong type carries identity before photos are ready. |

## Client brand (from Instagram @kejivisuals_weddings)
- Wedding-led photo + video studio; vivid, saturated Yoruba traditional weddings (gele, aso-oke, agbada), pre-wedding and bridal portraits; mostly portrait orientation; IG images carry a "KEJI VISUALS" watermark.
- Existing identity: geometric logo mark + KEJI VISUALS wordmark; cream ~#EFEDE0, olive-gold ~#A9A06B, black; Cinzel-style serif caps with two-tone headlines; Montserrat-style body; film-reel/clapperboard clip-art (we replace with a refined film-strip motif).
- His copy used in the mockup: "Behind the lens" bio, four values, service one-liners, "Ready to tell your story?". Educational post "Four questions to ask your wedding photographer before booking" → "Before you book" FAQ.

## Motion research — recent.design (web category)
Adopted (adapted, not copied): masked text rise + crop-mark brackets (Skale footer), scrolling name marquee (Sam Cheng personal site).
Later candidates: pinned "How booking works" section (Calendly pinned scroll); calm ribbon carousel for selected stories (Fallengrey Studio).
Rejected for Keji: WebGL orbs/halo/glass (heavy on mobile data, upstage the photos), crumpled-paper unfold (gimmicky).
