# Keji Visuals Website: What We Need From You

Hi Keji, here's everything needed to build your new website. Send what you have; we can start with the photos and brand items while you work on the written content.

## 1. Brand
- Logo, ideally SVG or high-resolution PNG with a transparent background
- Brand colours and fonts, if you have them
- 2–3 websites you like, and a sentence on what you like about each

## 2. Photos
**Export settings (important):** JPEG, sRGB colour, long edge 3000–4000 px, quality around 85%. Please don't send RAW files or full-size originals. Remove location (GPS) data on export.

- **Hero images:** 8–15 of your absolute best, mixing landscape and portrait orientation
- **Galleries:** for each category you want to show (e.g. weddings, portraits, events, maternity, commercial), 15–40 images grouped into named sessions. Example: "Tolu & Femi — Wedding, Lagos, 2026"
- **About photo:** a portrait of you, ideally at work
- **Permissions:** please confirm you have your clients' permission to publish each session, especially for children and private events

## 3. Packages & rates
For each package:
- Name and category
- Price in naira (plus pounds or dollars if you serve clients abroad)
- How it should show: exact price, "from" price, or "enquire for pricing"
- Session length, number of edited images, and delivery turnaround
- What's included, and any optional add-ons with prices

Also tell us:
- Travel or location fees
- Your deposit and cancellation policy

## 4. Written content
- About you: your story, how you got into photography, and how you work with clients
- Your style in 2–3 sentences
- A short description of each service
- 3–8 testimonials with the client's name (and permission to use them)
- Common questions clients ask you, with your answers

## 5. Contact & practical details
- The email address enquiries should go to
- WhatsApp number for click-to-chat
- Instagram and other social links
- Where you're based and where you'll travel to
- What you'd like the enquiry form to ask (e.g. type of shoot, date, location, budget)
- Your domain name, if you own one, and who it's registered with. If you don't have one, we'll help you buy it.
- If you use Pixieset, Pic-Time or similar for client galleries, the link

## How to send
Share a Google Drive or Dropbox folder with one sub-folder per category or gallery, plus a document for the text.
